The idea behind Quest

Every big goal is a mountain.

From the bottom you cannot see the top. Halfway up the fog rolls in, the trail dips before it climbs, and it is hard to tell whether you are moving at all. A quest is the map for that climb: the summit you chose, the stretches between, and where you are right now. Quest AI walks beside you, so a bad week is a dip in the trail, not the end of the climb.
