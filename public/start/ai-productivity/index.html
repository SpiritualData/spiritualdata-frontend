Who this is for

Built for a goal that takes months.

Quest is built around a quest: one big goal of yours, turned into a plan of yearly, quarterly, monthly, weekly and daily goals, plus habits and a daily reflection. Your AI rep is an AI that works on your behalf, from that plan, and it keeps what you teach it.

Quest is for the person with a long project who is tired of re-explaining it, and who wants the AI to carry more of the work as it earns trust, with every step visible and reversible.

You get a plan that lasts, a rep that keeps what you teach it, and a view of everything it did.

01 · It learns from you

Correct it once. Read what it learned. Edit or delete it.

Your rep learns two things. How to respond: from a thumbs down with a note, a critique in your next message, or an “Improve this reply” in a team chat. And how to decide: every time you approve, decline or pass on an ask, with your own words, it can fold the reason into a short guidance line. Both are plain sentences you can read.

You tell it, once, what was wrong.

In Quest AI chat you give a reply a thumbs down and add a note. Example wording: “Too much. Just the goals.” You can also say it in your next message, or react with a thumbs down in a team chat.

Question: What should I focus on this week?

Learned

How you want replies shaped, which sources to consult, and how you decide approvals. It can also learn how to draft an answer for you when you rewrite its first draft.

Kept where it belongs

Facts about your life stay in your data, and bug reports go in a report. The rep keeps a lesson only when it is sure.

#Capped, so you can read it

Up to 50 notes, 50 context preferences and 60 decision guidances per rep. A lesson is a sentence a language model wrote from your feedback.

How AI reps work

A scoreboard for your rep

Each rep page sums up the week: first passes prepared, decisions made and lessons learned, with the latest lesson underneath and all activity by week, month and total one tap away. Each lesson shows how often it was used, and you can edit or delete any of them.

02 · It decides what to bring to you, in one narrow place

Small approvals can be settled for you. Everything else still reaches you.

When an AI raises an “approve this?” ask, your rep first checks it against three things: the instruction you wrote, its decision guidance, and your own earlier answers to similar asks. If they clearly show how you would answer, it settles the ask and tells you why. If it is unsure, the ask stays in your list.

What it can settle

Plain “approve” asks raised by an AI. It is on by default and needs at least 85% confidence. You can switch it off, and you can write an instruction such as “always show me anything about money or publishing”.

What it never settles

Asks a person made to you directly, workflow steps, and anything that would run a prepared action once approved.

Every one can be undone

Each settled ask lists the earlier answers it relied on. Undo reopens it, and your next answer teaches the rep that this kind of ask should reach you.

Your voice on the rest

For asks that do reach you, your rep can draft the first answer in your voice. When you edit the draft, it learns from the difference.

It judges one kind of ask, from your words and your history, and it shows its working.

03 · Hand it work that runs on your own setup

An AI task runs where the work lives, and is checked against a goal you wrote.

Create a task from your daily reflection, the Delegate to AI form, Quest AI chat, or by assigning a goal to your AI rep. It can run once or repeat. You can pause it, steer it while it works, follow up on the result, or undo it.

Pick a kind of task and see where it runs.

Your machine

Web research needs a connected quest-ai-runner. It runs on a computer you control, so what it reads and which keys it uses stay with you.

The runner is yours

Web research and code tasks need quest-ai-runner connected, on a computer you control. For deep work it can start Claude Code with your own Claude login, picks a smaller or larger model by how hard the job is, and checks the result against the written goal. If the check fails it retries or moves up a model. If it needs you, it raises a decision. If your own usage limit is reached, it treats that as a pause.

A team can register several runner environments with the abilities each has (web, a document corpus, code), and Quest shows whether each is alive.

04 · Context you can read, and fork

The engine that manages context is open source.

quest-ai-runner is the same open-source project that Quest AI uses to orchestrate chat and tasks, under the Apache-2.0 licence. Read how it chooses what the model sees, run it, change it.

What is in it

  • Context cards. Small summaries of your files, notes and conversations. Cards for code are built from docstrings with no model call; retrieval mixes semantic vectors with keyword (BM25) search.
  • A sufficiency check. Before answering, a checklist asks whether the context gathered is enough.
  • One filter pass, and a card store capped at 1,000 cards by default, with a notice when it is full.
  • Many sources. Files, databases, vector stores, past conversations and the web.

One measured figure: in a modelled scenario, the opening listing of a typical 1,000-file repo used about 62% fewer tokens.

Version 0.1.0, Python 3.10 or newer, installed from a clone with pip install -e . (it is not on PyPI). The project describes itself as pre-release, built first for Quest, and its API may change. Contact: opensource@spiritualdata.org.

How Quest AI uses your context

05 · You hold the controls

More autonomy only when you grant it, and a way back when it goes wrong.

Confirm before it acts

When Quest AI wants to change your data, it asks first with a card you approve or decline. Changes to your quests and goals ask first by default.

Auto-run, with a snapshot

Turn Auto-run on and it works without asking. It takes a snapshot first, so Undo restores your data. Some changes cannot be undone automatically, and Quest says so on the result.

Automatic only if you say so

After five approvals in a row of the same kind of action, the AI may ask “Make it automatic?”. One rejection or one undo steps it back to asking, and creating a quest is never automatic.

Pause, steer, stop

Pause a task, steer it while it runs, follow up on the result, or press Stop AI. A delegate can answer your asks only.

06 · What it all works against

A plan that is still there in month eight.

Everything above works against your quest: one big goal broken into a year, quarters, months, weeks and days. Your rep learns inside it, your tasks are checked against it, and each quest keeps its own context. It is deliberately a slow tool for a long goal.

Plans connect to the rest of your day too: two-way Google Calendar sync, web search in Quest AI chat, email in and out of Quest, scheduling links, and a REST API with your own keys.
