Meet Quest AI

Your AI guide, built into every Quest and into your team’s chat. It works from the same shared plan your team sees. It can move plans and progress forward, but it never changes the goal or marks a quest complete: those stay with people.

The idea behind Quest

One shared plan, with an owner on every part.

Picture a team where everyone keeps their own list. Nobody can see the whole picture, so things fall between people and nobody notices until a deadline passes. Quest gives the team one shared plan for a goal that takes months. Every part of it has a named person, anything without one is easy to spot, and Quest AI reads the same plan everyone else does.
