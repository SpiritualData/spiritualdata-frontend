The idea behind Quest

One thread through everything you practice.

A session, a retreat, a quiet morning: each gives you something worth keeping. Left alone, they stay separate and fade by midweek. Quest strings them together on one thread: the intent you named, the habits that keep it alive and the insights you capture. You stay one conscious self, keeping the same intentions going for months instead of starting over every week.
