In plain words

A quest is one big goal turned into a plan. A team shares it. A workflow is the diagram of who does what, and when, every week. AI reps do the steps an AI can do, and a person stays accountable for the goal.

01 · One plan, an owner at every level

The whole plan, from the year down to today.

Share a quest with your team and everyone sees the same plan, year to day. Each goal and habit is assigned to one person and lands on their Today. Leave one unassigned and a teammate can take it in one tap. Members move their own goals forward; only owners and admins change the goal itself or complete the quest.

AI reps are never the named owner. The person stays accountable, and their rep can do the work if they allow it (see hand-overs).

02 · Workflows

Write the weekly routine down once. Quest keeps it moving.

Most teams have a routine that lives in one person’s head: the Monday summary, the numbers from the field, the sign-off, the recap. A workflow turns it into steps with an owner, a day and a time window each. A step is done by a person or by one of your AI reps, and arrows show what waits on what.

Describe it, get a draft

Say in a sentence how your team works and Quest AI drafts the workflow. A draft never runs until people accept it.

Ask AI to change this

Ask for a change in words. You see a preview of what is changed, added and removed before anything is applied.

Approvals become Asks

An approve or deny step appears in the approver’s Asks for You, and the step is done when they answer.

Done from what people already do

A step can be marked done when the assigned person posts a goal update or a quest note, or finishes their weekly review. No extra clicking.

Risk, quietly

One calm line when all is on track. When not, it counts what is late or missed, says what each one holds up, and offers Nudge, Snooze or Reassign.

Starting points

Begin from “Weekly review” (an AI rep briefs the team on Monday and people reply) or “Approval chain” (a person drafts, a person approves, an AI rep posts). Both need an AI rep on the team.

What is still maturing

  • Scheduled AI steps are the newest part, and they need a connected quest-ai-runner. They are tested, but not yet proven end to end on real teams, so keep a person on any step that matters.
  • A step’s status (Open, Done, Late, Missed) is worked out from your records and the clock, not typed in by hand.
  • We have not finished testing workflows on real phones, so use a laptop or a larger screen for the diagram today.
  • A step can ask an AI rep to run, ask a person to decide, or remind a person to mark it done. It does not reach into other tools for you.

03 · Asks, and what your rep may do for you

Approvals that arrive drafted, and only as often as you allow.

An ask is a request for your yes, no or pass, from a teammate, a workflow step or Quest AI. All of them land in one list called Asks for You, and each one can carry a deadline and a default for what happens if you say nothing. Your AI rep is the AI that works for you: it drafts the first answer in your voice, and it learns from what you decide.

You choose how much reaches you

  1. 1Every ask reaches youApprove, decline or pass, with your own words if you want to add any.
  2. 2Your rep drafts firstIt writes a first answer in your voice. You use it, edit it, or ignore it.
  3. 3Edits teach draftingWhen your answer differs from the draft, your rep learns how to draft for you next time. You can see and remove what it learned.

An explainer of the two settings. In the app the setting is a switch plus an instruction box in the “Settled for you” sheet.

It learns from you, and shows its work.

Your approvals, declines and passes, with the words you give, become short written lessons on your rep. A thumbs-down with a note in chat does the same. Facts and bug reports are deliberately not learned.

Settling asks for you is narrow on purpose. It is limited to low-stakes “approve” asks raised by AI, never asks a person sent you directly, never workflow steps, and never anything that would run a prepared action. After five approvals in a row of the same kind of action, Quest AI may offer to stop asking, as a question you answer. Nothing turns on silently.

04 · Hand-overs that keep the story

When a goal changes hands, the reason goes with it.

Assigning a goal to someone else adds an update to the goal that says who handed it over, why it matters and what the next step is. The new owner is told. AI tasks that have not started move to their AI rep.

Who does an assigned goal

Each person decides what their AI rep may do with goals assigned to them (Settings, AI Tasks, “When a goal is assigned to me”):

  • Let my AI rep take it when it can

    Only goals an AI can do completely, such as drafting a document. You see a “Your AI rep took this” note and can take the goal back at any time. This is the default.

  • Ask me first

    Each time, you choose: let your AI handle it, or do it yourself.

  • Never

    Assigned goals always stay with you.

05 · The team chat and the AI work behind it

Ask in the chat. The work happens where it can.

Team chat with Quest AI

Mention the AI assistant in your team chat and it answers from the same shared plan: what everyone is working on, which goals are overdue and who owns them. Under an AI reply, “Improve this reply” teaches your rep, and “Make this a task” hands it to your AI to do in the background. The AI never changes the goal or marks a quest complete: those stay with people.

How Quest AI works in teams

AI tasks, where they run

Tasks that work on your Quest data or read Quest’s own docs run inside Quest. Tasks that need the web, or that write or run code, run on your team’s own runner. They can be one-off or repeating, you can pause one, steer it while it runs, ask a follow-up, or undo it. Changes to your quests ask you first unless you switch on Auto-run, which takes a snapshot so you can undo.

How AI tasks work

Environments your team connects

A team registers the machines that run its AI work and says what each can do (web, documents, code). Quest shows whether each one is live. The runner is quest-ai-runner, open source under Apache-2.0. It runs on your own computer with your own Claude login or key, so Quest does not host it for you. It is pre-release software and its interfaces may change, and you install it from a clone of the repository.
