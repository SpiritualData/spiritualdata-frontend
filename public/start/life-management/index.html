The idea behind Quest

Be the hero who gets to everything.

Work, home, health, money: they all call at once, and a hero cannot let one rescue stop the others. Quest keeps the whole map, every quest, goal and deadline in one place, and tells you who to save next. You stay one conscious self, doing the thing in front of you, while Quest handles the switching between everything else.
